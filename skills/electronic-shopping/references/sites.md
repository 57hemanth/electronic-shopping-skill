# Where to check, and what to look for

Check **at least two** of these for every shortlisted model. Use the exact product page, not a search
results page. Record: site, price paid without a specific bank card, the variant, the URL, today's date,
delivery (yes / no / not checked) with the pincode the store checked, and offers separately. Never
change the account's saved address; see SKILL.md "Delivery and pincode".

## Marketplaces
| Site | Check | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Amazon.in** | Price, seller name (prefer the brand or Appario / Cloudtail-style large sellers), "Deliver to" pincode, delivery date, installation (TV/AC), return window | Third-party sellers with low ratings; "Renewed" listings mixed in; colour or storage variants priced differently |
| **Flipkart** | Price, seller, "Enter delivery pincode", delivery date, installation, exchange value | "Super Value" or bundle listings that change the variant; exchange discounts shown in the headline price |

## Brand stores (India sites)
samsung.com/in · mi.com/in · oneplus.in · apple.com/in · store.google.com (Pixel) · dell.com/en-in ·
hp.com/in-en · lenovo.com/in · asus.com/in · lg.com/in · sony.co.in · daikinindia.com · voltas.com ·
bluestarindia.com
- Check for **student, corporate and exchange offers**, which marketplaces don't have.
- Use them for **official specs** and the **service-centre locator**.
- Prices on brand stores are sometimes higher than marketplaces; sometimes they're the only place a
  variant is in stock.

## Large chains (online and in store)
Croma · Reliance Digital · Vijay Sales
- Useful for ACs and TVs (installation teams) and for checking in-store pickup near the buyer.

## Local chains in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh
Big C · Happi Mobiles · Lot Mobiles · Sangeetha Mobiles · B New Mobiles (mobiles); Bajaj Electronics
(electronics and appliances), and town-level dealers.
- Many buyers in towns and villages buy here. Offline prices, exchange offers and EMI schemes can beat
  online, and the shop helps with setup.
- You usually can't confirm their prices online: tell the user to **show the best online price at the
  shop and ask them to match it**. Check a branch exists near the pincode using the chain's store locator.

## Offers (record separately from the price)
The full procedure, with where each store shows its offers, is the `shopping-offers` skill.
- **Bank card offers** (instant discounts on specific cards): note the card and amount.
- **No-cost EMI**: note the tenure; check for processing fees.
- **Exchange**: value depends on the old device; never include it in the main price.
- **Sale events**: Big Billion Days (Flipkart), Great Indian Festival (Amazon), Republic Day and
  Independence Day sales, festival sales around Diwali, Sankranti and Ugadi. If one is days away,
  say "wait until the sale" is an option.

## Service centres
Use the brand's official service locator page with the pincode or nearest town. Record: the town, and
the distance if shown. "Not found within 50 km" is important information; say it plainly.
