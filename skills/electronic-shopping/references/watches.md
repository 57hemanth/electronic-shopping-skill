# Watches

## First question: smartwatch, fitness band or analog watch?

## Budget ranges (₹)
Smartwatch: Under 3,000 · 3,000–8,000 · 8,000–20,000 · 20,000–40,000 · Above 40,000
Analog: Under 2,000 · 2,000–5,000 · 5,000–15,000 · Above 15,000

## Main use
| Use | What matters most |
| --- | --- |
| **Fitness / walking / running** | Step and heart-rate accuracy (reviews), GPS built in, battery, water resistance |
| **Calling and notifications** | Bluetooth calling quality, display brightness, phone app quality |
| **Health tracking for parents** | Simple use, large display, reliable heart rate; SpO2 and ECG only if the model's feature is certified for that use in India; say it is not a medical device |
| **Style / gift** | Design, strap, brand, warranty |
| **Kids** | Durability, simple, (for smart kids' watches) GPS and SOS |

## Must-asks
- Which phone do they use? **Apple Watch works only with an iPhone.** Some Samsung health features need a
  Samsung phone. Wear OS watches need Android.
- Daily charging OK, or must it last a week?
- Gift or own use?

## Specs to compare (smartwatch)
Display type and brightness · battery days (typical use, from reviews) · built-in GPS · sensors · water
resistance (ATM / IP) · Bluetooth calling · app and ecosystem · strap size · warranty and service.

## Specs to compare (analog)
Movement (quartz, automatic) · case material and size · glass (mineral, sapphire) · water resistance ·
warranty · authorised dealer.

## Default if the buyer doesn't know
Smartwatch for notifications and steps, 5–7 day battery, mid budget; say so.

## Traps
- **Budget smartwatches** show health numbers that can be far off; never present them as medical readings.
- **Fake "AMOLED" or brightness claims** on very cheap watches; check reviews.
- **Grey-market watches** on marketplaces: buy from the brand store or an authorised seller for warranty.
- **App quality**: the watch is only as good as its phone app; check app reviews.
