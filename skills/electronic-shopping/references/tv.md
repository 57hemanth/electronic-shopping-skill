# TVs

## Budget ranges (₹)
Under 15,000 · 15,000–25,000 · 25,000–40,000 · 40,000–70,000 · Above 70,000

## Must-asks
- **Room size and the distance from the sofa to the wall** (in feet). This decides the screen size.
- Mostly OTT apps (Netflix, Hotstar, Aha, YouTube), cable/DTH set-top box, or cricket/sports?
- Wall mount or table stand?
- Do they already have a sound bar or home theatre?

## Screen size from viewing distance (rule of thumb)
| Viewing distance | Size |
| --- | --- |
| Under 5 ft | 32" |
| 5–7 ft | 40–43" |
| 7–9 ft | 50–55" |
| 9–11 ft | 65" |
Say it is a rule of thumb; 32" is usually HD, and from 43" upwards 4K is the norm.

## Main use
| Use | What matters most |
| --- | --- |
| **OTT and family viewing** | Smart platform (Google TV, Tizen, webOS), Telugu content apps available, picture quality, sound |
| **Sports / cricket** | Motion handling, refresh rate, brightness |
| **Gaming (console)** | HDMI 2.1, 120 Hz, low input lag (ALLM / VRR) |
| **Bedroom / small room** | Size, price, smart features |
| **Parents** | Simple remote, easy interface, good sound, trusted service |

## Specs to compare
Size and resolution · panel type (LED, QLED, Mini-LED, OLED) · refresh rate (native, not "motion rate") ·
HDR formats · smart platform and **years of software updates** · speaker watts and Dolby Atmos · HDMI ports
and versions · RAM/storage for apps · warranty (panel warranty separately if listed).

## Default if the buyer doesn't know
Size from an 8 ft distance (50–55") if the budget allows, otherwise 43"; Google TV / Tizen / webOS.

## Traps
- **"4K" at very low prices** can mean a weak processor and dim panel; check reviews for picture quality.
- **Installation and wall-mount charges**: free installation may exclude the bracket. Check at the pincode.
- **Voltage fluctuations**: recommend a surge protector or stabilizer where power is unstable.
- **Sound**: thin TVs often have weak speakers; mention a budget sound bar if sound matters.
- **Same model name, different sizes** can have different panels; compare the exact size listing.
