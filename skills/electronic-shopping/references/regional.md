# Telangana and Andhra Pradesh specifics

## Heat
- Summer temperatures reach 45 °C and above in many districts (Ramagundam, Khammam, Nalgonda, Vijayawada,
  Ongole, Nellore and others). Phones and laptops throttle in heat; mention cooling for gaming buyers.
- ACs: size up for top floors and afternoon sun (see `ac.md`); prefer models rated to cool at high
  ambient temperatures.

## Power
- Voltage fluctuations and cuts are common in rural areas. For ACs and TVs: check the operating voltage
  range, and suggest a stabilizer or surge protector when the buyer says power is unstable.
- Laptops: battery life matters more where power cuts are common.

## Coastal Andhra
- Salty, humid air corrodes AC condenser coils and outdoor units faster (Visakhapatnam, Kakinada,
  Machilipatnam, Ongole, Nellore and the coastal belt). Prefer anti-corrosion coated copper coils.

## Service and delivery
- Service centres are concentrated in district towns. For a village pincode, find the nearest town with
  an authorised centre and say how far it is. Prefer brands with a centre in the buyer's district.
- Delivery to rural pincodes can take longer, and installation (AC, TV) may be scheduled days later.
  Check both on the product page with the pincode.

## How people buy here
- Many buyers compare online but buy at a local shop for EMI, exchange and help with setup. The advice
  should work for both: give the online price as a reference to bargain with.
- Phones are often bought for parents by children working in Hyderabad, Bengaluru or abroad: the buyer
  and the user can be different people. Ask who will use it.
- Telugu OTT apps (Aha, ETV Win) matter for TV buyers: check the TV's app store supports them, or that a
  streaming stick does.
