# Brand tiers

**These are the skill owner's opinions, not facts.** Edit this file to match your own experience and
what people in your area trust. When you present a tier to a buyer, say "commonly trusted" or "value
brand", never "best". After-sales service quality varies by town: always check the service centre
near the buyer's pincode, whatever the tier.

- **Trusted** = established names with wide service networks and steady quality.
- **Value / power** = more specs for the money; service and software polish vary more.
