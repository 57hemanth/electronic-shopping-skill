# Laptops

## Budget ranges (₹)
Under 35,000 · 35,000–50,000 · 50,000–70,000 · 70,000–1,00,000 · Above 1,00,000

## Main use
| Use | What matters most | Minimum to recommend |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Coding** (B.Tech, developers) | RAM, CPU, SSD, keyboard, display, battery, Linux/WSL friendly | 16 GB RAM (8 GB only under ₹40,000, and say it will feel slow with an IDE + browser + Docker), 512 GB SSD, recent i5 / Ryzen 5 class |
| **Interviews and online classes** | 1080p webcam, microphones, speakers, battery, Wi-Fi 6, light weight | 8 GB RAM is acceptable, 16 GB better; webcam 1080p preferred |
| **Basic use** (office, browsing, YouTube, documents, parents) | Reliability, SSD (never a hard disk), battery, service | 8 GB RAM, 256–512 GB SSD |
| **Gaming** | Dedicated GPU (RTX class), CPU, cooling, high refresh display, upgradeable RAM | 16 GB RAM, RTX 3050/4050 class or better |
| **Design and video editing** | Colour-accurate display (100% sRGB or better), CPU/GPU, RAM | 16 GB RAM, good display |
| **Data science / AI learning** | RAM, GPU (for local models), storage | 16 GB RAM; say cloud notebooks (Colab, Kaggle) cover most learning |

## Must-asks
- Carried daily (college, office) or stays on a desk? Weight and battery matter much more if carried.
- Windows or Mac? (Suggest a MacBook only when budget and use fit, and the buyer wants macOS.)
- Any software their college or job requires (some need Windows-only tools)?

## Specs to compare
CPU model and generation · RAM amount **and whether it is soldered or upgradeable** · SSD size and slots ·
display (size, resolution, panel type, brightness in nits, sRGB coverage) · GPU · battery Wh · weight ·
webcam resolution · ports (USB-C charging, HDMI) · **warranty: years, onsite or carry-in**, accidental
damage option · OS and MS Office included or not.

## Default if the buyer doesn't know
"Basic use" plus light coding for students; 16 GB RAM if the budget allows, and say so.

## Traps
- **8 GB soldered RAM** can never be upgraded: it ages the laptop fastest. Call it out.
- **Dim 250-nit, 45% NTSC displays** on budget laptops look washed out. Mention brightness and colour.
- **Old-generation CPUs** sold at "discount" prices: compare generation, not just "i5".
- **Student / education offers** from brand stores can beat Amazon and Flipkart: check them.
- **Carry-in warranty** means taking it to a service centre; check where the nearest one is.
