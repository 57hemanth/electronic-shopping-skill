# ACs

## Budget ranges (₹)
Under 30,000 · 30,000–40,000 · 40,000–50,000 · 50,000–65,000 · Above 65,000
(Mention installation and copper pipe extension costs separately.)

## Must-asks
- **Room size** in feet (length × width), and ceiling height if unusual.
- **Floor**: top floor (terrace above) or not?
- **Afternoon sun** on the room's walls or windows (west-facing)?
- **How many hours a day**, and day or night use? This decides whether the star rating pays off.
- **Location**: coastal Andhra (salty air) or inland?
- Window AC or split AC?
- Voltage problems at home?

## Tonnage from room size (rule of thumb for Telangana / Andhra summers)
| Room size | Tonnage |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 120 sq ft | 1 ton |
| 120–180 sq ft | 1.5 ton |
| 180–250 sq ft | 2 ton |
Go one step up for a **top floor, strong afternoon sun, a kitchen next to the room or more than 3–4
people** regularly. Summers here reach 45 °C; an undersized AC runs flat out and still doesn't cool.

## Main use
| Use | What matters most |
| --- | --- |
| **Long daily use (8+ hours)** | Inverter, higher star rating (5-star pays back faster), efficiency |
| **Night use, bedroom** | Low noise (dB), sleep mode, inverter |
| **Occasional use** | 3-star inverter is usually enough; lower purchase price matters more |
| **Coastal Andhra** | Anti-corrosion coating on the condenser coil, copper coils |
| **Low voltage areas** | Wide operating voltage range, or a stabilizer |

## Specs to compare
Tonnage · inverter or fixed speed · **BEE star rating and annual energy consumption in kWh** (on the BEE
label) · ISEER value · **copper or aluminium condenser coil** · anti-corrosion coating · rated cooling at
high ambient temperature (some brands state cooling at 52 °C or higher) · noise dB · refrigerant · warranty
(product, PCB, compressor years separately) · installation included or not.

## Electricity bill estimate
Annual kWh from the BEE label assumes a fixed number of hours; scale it to the buyer's hours. Multiply by
the household tariff of their discom (Telangana: TGSPDCL, TGNPDCL; Andhra Pradesh: APEPDCL, APSPDCL,
APCPDCL). Look up the current slab on the discom's site; slabs mean the extra units are charged at the
buyer's highest slab. Show it as "about ₹X a month in summer" and say it is an estimate.

## Default if the buyer doesn't know
1.5 ton, 3-star inverter, copper coil; say so.

## Traps
- **Installation extras**: copper pipe beyond the included length, outdoor-unit stand, drain pipe, and
  "free installation" that isn't free. Ask the seller and mention typical extras.
- **Installation availability at the pincode**: brand or store installation may not reach smaller towns
  quickly. Check.
- **Old stock**: last year's star rating labels differ. Check the year on the BEE label.
- **"Convertible 5-in-1"** is marketing: it just runs at lower capacity.
- **Window ACs** are cheaper and simpler to install but louder.
